## Features & Content
* **History:** The origins of the name Bandung based on Sundanese legends and philosophy.
* **Geography:** Details on the city's basin morphology and main river systems.
* **Tourist Destinations:** Information on popular spots like Farm House Lembang and Bosscha Observatory.
* **General Information:** Quick facts including the founding date, total area, local language, and telephone code.
